The spice must flow

Arrakis Spice Labels

List your spices, pick a design, press print. Jar labels 60×30 mm, lid labels Ø27 or Ø30 mm, box labels 70×70 mm — laid out on A4.

Design

Print

Lid style

In the print dialog choose A4, scale 100 % (not “fit to page”), margins none, and turn on background graphics.

Spices

One spice per line. Write Name / variant (e.g. Paprika / søt) for the small line under the name, and optionally Name / variant / origin. Per category, tick which labels to print: “jar” and “lid” for every spice in it, and “box” for one box label (with a row per spice to write the grams on). They are independent. Spices are numbered by category (Urter 101, 102 …, Chili 201 …), so adding a spice — best at the end of a list — never renumbers other categories. The Botanisk design adds the Latin name and plant family for common spices (built in).
